Fit-to-Work Examinations

Capability assessment prior to employment, task change or return after illness. Evaluates physical, psychological and functional readiness through medical history, organ systems review, spirometry, audiometry and drug screening.

Deliverable: Fitness Certificate (Fit / Fit with Restrictions / Unfit)

Benefits: Accident prevention, DOSHS/OSHA compliance, reduced liability exposure.

Exit Medical Examinations

Documents health status on leaving employment or project completion. Screens for occupational exposures, injuries and latent conditions via exposure history, targeted chest X-rays, blood panels and exit physicals.

Deliverable: Exit Health Record & Occupational Disease Assessment Report

Benefits: Protection against future claims and clear proof of duty of care.

  • Safety documentation review, employee & management interviews, facility inspections and daily protocol evaluation
  • Auditing & improving safe systems of work (Permit to Work, Task-Specific Risk Assessments)
  • Safety culture assessments (structured interviews + behaviour-based observations)
  • Statutory compliance (OSHA / DOSHS, NEMA)
  • Development & implementation of integrated HSE Management Systems aligned with ISO 45001 & ISO 14001

Custom modules, toolbox talks, safety leadership workshops and competency-based training.

Key topics include:

First Aid Manual Handling COSHH / GHS Working at Height LOTO Risk Assessment & JSA Incident Reporting

Deliverables: Training materials, attendance registers, post-training evaluations and certificates of competence.

Fire hazard evaluation and emergency response strategies covering combustible materials, ignition sources, detection & suppression systems, exit routes and drill plans.

  • • Statutory notification support

Routine, planned and targeted inspections covering hazard identification, control verification, machine guarding, PPE, fire systems, emergency equipment and exits. Includes review of previous corrective actions.

Deliverables: Comprehensive inspection reports, CAPA plans and compliance trend analysis.
