Fit-to-Work Examinations

Capability assessment prior to employment, task change or after illness. Includes physical, psychological & functional readiness; medical history; organ systems review; spirometry; audiometry; drug screening.

Deliverable: Fitness Certificate (Fit / Fit with Restrictions / Unfit).

Benefits: Accident prevention, DOSHS/OSHA compliance, reduced liability.

Exit Medical Examinations

Document health status on leaving or project completion. Screening for occupational exposures, injuries and latent conditions. Exposure history, targeted chest X-rays, blood panels, exit physicals.

Deliverable: Exit Health Record & Occupational Disease Assessment Report.

Benefits: Protection against future claims and clear proof of duty of care.

  • Safety documentation review, employee & management interviews, facility inspections, daily protocol evaluation
  • Auditing & improving safe systems of work (Permit to Work, Task-Specific Risk Assessments)
  • Safety culture assessments (interviews + behaviour-based observations)
  • Statutory compliance (OSHA / DOSHS, NEMA)
  • Development & implementation of integrated HSE Management Systems aligned with ISO 45001 & ISO 14001

  • Incident Reporting & Investigation

Deliverables: Training materials, attendance registers, post-training evaluations, certificates.

Fire hazard evaluation and emergency response strategies covering combustible materials & ignition sources, detection/suppression systems, exit routes and drill plans.
